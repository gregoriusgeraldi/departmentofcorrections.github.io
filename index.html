How to Use 10-Codes / Response Codes on Radio

Pick up Request

  • Dispatch, 41-Bravo-151, 76 MRPD picking up 2 95 at Alta HQ with code 2, ETA 5 minutes. stand by for updates.
  • Dispatch, 41-Bravo-151, merging to radio for prisoner pickup.
  • Dispatch, 41-Bravo-151, 23 at MRPD. Beginning prisoner transfer process.
  • Dispatch, 41-Bravo-151, 95 received, departing from MRPD, 76 BB, ETA 5 minutes. stand by for updates.

    • Escorting Prisoners to Bolingbroke

    Dispatch, 41-Bravo-151, 76 BB escorting 2 95 at Alta HQ with code 3, ETA 5 minutes. stand by for updates.

    High Risk Transport

  • Dispatch, 44-Bravo-034, 76 DOJ escorting 1 high-risk 95. Requesting secondary unit.
  • Dispatch, 44-Bravo-034, transporting 3 95 to BB. Code 3 response.
  • Dispatch, 44-Bravo-034, convoy established. 76 BB with 8 95, stand by.

    • Transport Delay

  • Dispatch, 42-Bravo-031, 77 on Route 13. Vehicle issue, transport delayed.
  • Dispatch, 42-Bravo-031, traffic congestion on Olympic Freeway. Updated ETA 15 minutes to BB.
  • Dispatch, 42-Bravo-031, stopping for prisoner welfare check. Standby future update.

    • Emergency

  • Dispatch, 41-Bravo-151, 78. Prisoner disturbance inside transport vehicle.
  • Dispatch, 41-Bravo-151, 78 at Route 68. Immediate assistance required.
  • Dispatch, 41-Bravo-151, 71. Shots fired at transport unit.
  • Dispatch, 41-Bravo-151, 13A. Officer down. Requesting Code 3 response.

    • Preferred Update Structure

    • Action — heading / belok kiri / belok kanan
    • Direction — North / South / East / West
    • Street Name — lokasi atau jalur
    • Additional Information — landmark, lalu lintas, deskripsi suspect
    Heading West on Vespucci Blvd, taking left at intersection, heading South on San Andreas Ave. Light traffic, occupied by 2 suspects.

    Incident Commander

    Seorang IC harus tegas, sigap, menguasai situasi, berani mengambil keputusan, dan tidak takut menegur. IC bukan sekadar role, tapi tanggung jawab penuh atas jalannya sebuah insiden.
